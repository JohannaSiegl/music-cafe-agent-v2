# Vinyl Music Café - Café Rules and Policies
# Class practice data. These rules are made up for a class project; they are not the rules of a real café.
# Opening hours, prices, payment, address, phone, delivery, parking, Wi-Fi and the queue for song requests are in the café database, not in this file.

## About the café and seating
Vinyl Music Café is a small café with coffee, tea, desserts, sandwiches, vinyl music and occasional live performances.
Our guests are mostly students, music lovers and café visitors, and everyone is welcome.
The café has 12 tables. Each table has 2 or 4 seats.
Walk-in guests are welcome whenever seats are free. A reservation is not required.

## Table reservations
Tables can be reserved for 1 to 4 people. A reservation is for one table.
To reserve a table, the guest gives the day, the time of arrival, the number of people, a name and a phone number.
Tables can be reserved up to 14 days in advance, and at least 2 hours before the time of arrival.
The time of arrival must be within our opening hours.
A reservation lasts 2 hours. A reserved table is held for 15 minutes after the booked time.
Groups of more than 4 people cannot reserve a table directly. They must contact the staff.
A reservation counts as confirmed only after the guest has agreed to the booking details and the booking has been saved in our system. The guest then receives a reservation number, which is needed to cancel.
To change a reservation, the guest cancels it and makes a new one.

## Cancellations and no-shows
Guests can cancel a reservation free of charge up to 3 hours before the time of arrival.
To cancel, the guest needs the reservation number and the phone number of the booking.
A cancellation less than 3 hours before the time of arrival must be made with the staff.
If a reserved table is not used and the guest did not cancel, a no-show fee of NT$150 applies.

## Tables, QR codes and free seats
Every table has a QR code. Guests scan it when they sit down, and the table is marked as occupied in our system.
The staff mark the table as free again after the guests have left.
The number of free tables and seats can be checked at any time.

## Friday live music
Live music is played every Friday from 19:00 to 21:30.
Five performers take turns: Lin Yu-Chen (acoustic guitar and vocals), Chen Wei-Ting (piano), Huang Mei-Ling (jazz vocals), Wu Jia-Hao (violin) and Zhang Xiao-Yun (folk guitar).
The order of the performers changes from week to week. Who plays when is shown on the poster and the schedule posted in the café.
Each event has limited seating capacity. Guests should check availability before visiting.
Guests may take photos, but flash photography is not allowed during live performances.

## Live music fee
Guests who are in the café during the live music on Fridays, from 19:00 to 21:30, pay a live music fee of NT$100 per person.
If the order of a table is worth at least NT$500, the live music fee is waived for that table.
If the fee applies, it is added to the bill at the end of the visit.

## Music in the café
Playing loud music of your own, for example from a phone or a speaker, at the table is not allowed.
Instead, guests can make a song request at any time. The staff will find the matching record and play it at the next possible time.

## House rules
Outside food and drinks are not allowed.
Smoking is prohibited inside the café.
Guests are expected to respect other customers and to keep the noise at a reasonable level.
Pets are not allowed, except certified assistance animals.

## Food and allergens
Our kitchen uses milk, eggs, wheat, soy and nuts every day.
We cannot promise that any item is completely free of these ingredients.
Guests with a serious allergy should tell the staff before ordering.
The vegetarian sandwich contains no meat.

## Wrong or cold orders and complaints
If an item is wrong or arrives cold, please tell the staff and we will make it again free of charge.
For other complaints, please speak to the staff or call the café.

## Lost and found
Items left in the café are kept for 30 days.
Wallets, phones and documents are kept in a safe place and need photo ID to collect.
After 30 days, unclaimed items are given to a local charity.
